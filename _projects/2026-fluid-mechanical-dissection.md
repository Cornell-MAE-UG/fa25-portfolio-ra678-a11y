This project involved a hands-on fluid-mechanical dissection focused on understanding how a physical system moves fluid and converts energy through mechanical components. By examining the design, operation, and interaction of the parts, the work connected real-world engineering behavior to the fundamental principles of fluid mechanics and machine design.

The analysis centered on how flow, pressure, and mechanical motion work together within the system, highlighting the importance of component geometry, material function, and system-level performance.
