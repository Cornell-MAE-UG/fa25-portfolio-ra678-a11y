This portfolio assignment tasked students with designing a lifting mechanism that could raise the maximum weight to the highest possible height within a constrained space. Working with a 2D design area of 150cm × 50cm, students had to strategically place three pin supports (with two mounted on the ground), choose an appropriate rigid bar length, and select a linear actuator from a provided catalog.
The challenge involved balancing two competing goals: lifting as much weight as possible while also achieving maximum height. Students applied core mechanical engineering principles like force analysis and mechanical advantage to optimize their designs. With all components assumed to be rigid, the focus was on finding the best geometric configuration and selecting the right actuator for the job. This project helped develop practical skills in working with design constraints and applying fundamental statics and mechanics concepts to real-world problems.
